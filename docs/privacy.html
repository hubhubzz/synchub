SyncHub

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

SyncHub is a command-line program that runs on your computer. It starts the separate rclone program to copy files from a folder you choose to a Google Drive destination you choose.

Information and files

SyncHub does not include an account service, analytics, or a SyncHub-operated server. The SyncHub program does not send usage data to its maintainers. The files you select are transferred by rclone to the Google Drive account configured in rclone. Google and rclone may process information as described in their own terms and privacy notices.

Credentials

Google Drive authorization is handled by rclone and stored in rclone's local configuration. SyncHub does not read, collect, or transmit your Google password or OAuth token separately. Protect your computer and rclone configuration as you would other credentials.

Website

This informational website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data under its own privacy terms.

Contact

For questions, contact the maintainers through SyncHub's GitHub Issues.

This policy describes the current project design. Review it whenever the app, website hosting, or data practices change.
