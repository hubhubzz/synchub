SyncHub

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

SyncHub is a command-line program that runs on your computer. It connects directly to Google Drive through the Google Drive API and uploads files from the local folder you choose to the Drive folder created for SyncHub.

Information and files

SyncHub does not include an account service, analytics, or a SyncHub-operated server. The app sends the Google account authorization token, file names, file metadata, and selected file contents directly from your device to Google Drive to perform the upload. SyncHub does not send this information to its maintainers. Google processes it under Google's own terms and privacy notices.

SyncHub requests Google's drive.file permission, which is limited to files the app creates or files you explicitly open or share with it. SyncHub creates and manages files within its own Drive folder.

Credentials

Google sign-in uses OAuth in your system browser. SyncHub never receives your Google password. It stores an OAuth refresh token in a configuration file on your device so it can continue syncing without asking you to sign in each time. On Unix-like systems, SyncHub restricts access to that file to your user account. You can revoke access from your Google Account security settings and remove the local token file from your operating system's SyncHub configuration directory.
