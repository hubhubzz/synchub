Local-first Google Drive sync

synchub

synchub is a Rust command-line app that uploads files from a local folder you choose to your Google Drive account. It runs on your computer and connects directly to Google Drive.

What synchub does

After you authorize Google Drive access, synchub creates or updates files in Drive/SyncHub/<folder-name>. It compares local files with files in its Drive folder, skips unchanged files, and does not delete Drive files when you remove local files. Symbolic links are skipped.

synchub requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. It uses that access only to provide the folder upload and update feature. File names, file metadata, file contents, and the OAuth token are handled by the app on your device and sent directly to Google as needed. synchub does not operate a server that receives or stores your Google Drive data.

synchub has no AI features. It does not use Google APIs or Google user data to create, request, or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII).

Preview a sync

synchub --dry-run ~/Documents

A dry run lists local files without connecting to Google. To sync, run synchub ~/Documents.
